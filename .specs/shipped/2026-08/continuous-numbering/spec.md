# Continuous numbering

## Chapters

Chapters are numbered continuously independently of acts. Ex:

Act I

Chapter 1: The beginning
Chapter 2: The early story

Act II

Chapter 3: The later story
Chapter 4: The end

This is reflected in:
- the table of contents
- the chapters list
- the story outline
- the epub TOC and chapter numbers
- other occurrences

## Scenes

Scenes are numbered continuously independently of chapters, but also have a chapter position/order so they can remain sortable.

This is reflected in:
- the story outline
- the scenes list (with a new column for chapter position)
  - other occurrences
