# Open questions

1. **Multi-book projects: switcher or current book only?** Recommend current book only. Other books stay reachable through search and recent scenes.
2. **Hide the act level when the book has one act?** Recommend yes. One "Act 1" heading adds a level with no information.
3. **Word counts?** Recommend chapter total on the chapter row only, muted (`x-word-count`). Data is free (`word_count` column). Scene rows stay name-only so they do not truncate more.
4. **Scene numbers on rows?** Recommend none. Book-wide "Scene 40" does not match "scene 2 of chapter 3"; the list order already shows the place.
5. **Recent section default?** Recommend collapsed. The outline becomes the main way to move; recent is one click away.
6. **Remember open chapters?** Recommend no (non-goal). Opening only the current chapter keeps the tree short on every load.
7. **Acts foldable too?** Recommend no. Closed chapters already keep a long book short.
