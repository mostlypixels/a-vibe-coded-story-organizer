Story codex · attribute planning

Short facts that change as the story moves

An attribute is one short value on an entry's sheet, held against the timeline — it takes effect at an event and stands until the next change. So the real work of attributes is current state: what a character wants, believes, knows, and is hiding right now.

What an attribute is

  • A short value. A word or a phrase — blue, injured, believes she is an orphan. Not a paragraph.
  • Mutable, most of the time. It's stored per event, so it can change across the book. That's the point, not a side effect.
  • About one entry. It describes this character, place, or group on its own.

What isn't an attribute

  • Backstory or prose. Origins, personality essays, history → the entry's Description, not an attribute.
  • A link to another entry. Employer, faction, ruler, a group's leader or members → a relationship. Test: if the value is really the name of another entry, it isn't an attribute.

Character

the current-state sheet

The heart of the codex. Most of a character's attributes are things that move — what drives them and what they hold true shifts scene to scene, and the timeline is built to record exactly that.

Mutable current state — the four that matter most

Current goal
What they're chasing in this stretch of the book.
e.g. Reach the capital before winter
Current beliefs
What they hold true right now — often wrong, often changing.
e.g. Believes her brother is dead
Current knowledge
What they actually know at this point. Drives dramatic irony.
e.g. Doesn't know the map is forged
Current lies
What they're pretending or hiding, and to whom.
e.g. Claims to be a merchant

Mutable other state that shifts

Mood
Emotional weather in the current scenes.
Health / condition
Well, injured, ill, exhausted.
Status
Alive, missing, presumed dead.
Standing / class
Their rank in society — can rise or fall.

By genre

Contemporary

  • Nationality Fixed
    Where they're from, as a short label.
  • Languages Fixed
    What they speak.

Historical

  • Form of address Mut.
    Honorific the period uses — shifts with marriage, rank, widowhood.
  • Affliction Mut.
    An era-typical ailment that comes and goes.

Fantasy

  • Species / race Fixed
    Human, elf, other.
  • Magical affinity Fixed
    The element or school they lean to.
  • Power level Mut.
    Grows or is drained through the story.
  • Curse / affliction Mut.
    A magical condition that can lift.

Science Fiction

  • Species Fixed
    Human, synthetic, alien.
  • Augmentations Mut.
    Implants added or lost over time.
  • Life-support need Mut.
    Air, gravity, meds they currently depend on.

Location

what it is · what state it's in

A place has a fixed nature and a changing condition. Who rules or lives there is a relationship, not an attribute — keep those off the sheet.
